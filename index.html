About Me

I'm Valentina, a product designer who believes in the power of restraint. Every element should earn its place. Every animation should have purpose.

Over the past 5 years, I've helped startups and established companies create digital experiences that users genuinely enjoy. From healthcare dashboards to streaming platforms, I approach each project with the same question: How can we make this simpler?

When I'm not designing, you'll find me exploring new cities, experimenting with generative art, or perfecting my espresso technique.
